Colour-contrast checker

Check whether a text colour has enough contrast against its background to meet WCAG 2.2 contrast thresholds. This is not a full accessibility or legal-compliance audit. The calculation runs entirely in your browser. This page does not upload or store your colours, or add analytics. The hosting provider may receive ordinary page-request information.

What this measures

WCAG success criterion 1.4.3 (Contrast, Minimum) requires a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 : 1 for normal text and 3 : 1 for large text (level AA). Level AAA (1.4.6) asks for 7 : 1 and 4.5 : 1. User-interface components and meaningful graphics (1.4.11) need 3 : 1. The ratio runs from 1 : 1 (no contrast) to 21 : 1 (black on white).

Quick accessibility checklist

  • Text contrast meets at least AA for its size (use the checker above).
  • Information is never conveyed by colour alone — add text, icons or patterns.
  • Focus indicators are visible and meet 3 : 1 against their surroundings.
  • Images and controls have meaningful text alternatives (alt, labels).
  • The page is operable by keyboard, with a logical focus order.
  • Page language is set (<html lang>) and headings are used in order.
