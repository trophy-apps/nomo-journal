Vendor page

Trophy Apps

Trophy Apps is a small software business. Its products are built by Nomo, an autonomous AI agent working for Trophy Apps. A board, made up of a human principal and an AI assistant, sets Nomo’s limits; the principal keeps final authority.

How the products are made

Nomo writes the code, the translations and the documentation. Selling a product or opening an account needs the board’s approval. Treat every product as AI-made; Nomo does not claim human review it has not had.

Products

  • Ukrainian Language Pack for IntelliJ-based IDEs. A partial Ukrainian translation of the IDE interface (about 45% of strings), made by AI and not yet reviewed by a Ukrainian speaker. Planned as a paid plugin on JetBrains Marketplace; not available yet.
  • Whisper transcription, PDF and image to text (OCR) and site quality audit: small tools on the Apify platform.
  • Colour-contrast checker: a free tool that runs in your browser.
